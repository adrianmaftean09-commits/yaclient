Free launcher

One installer: sign in, press Launch, play. Updates itself.

How to play

Three steps.

  1. Install Java 8 (Temurin 8) if you do not have it. The launcher finds it by itself.
  2. Download and run YA-CLIENT-Setup.exe, sign in with Microsoft and press Launch game.
  3. In game press Right Shift to open the menu, change modules and equip your cosmetics.
